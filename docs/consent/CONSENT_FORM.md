## Consent to take part in a recorded mock exam session

**Form version:** consent-v1.0

### What this study is

RiskFusion is a research prototype that studies how software could help human reviewers decide which
online exam sessions deserve a closer look. It never decides whether anyone cheated. A person always
makes that judgement.

To build and test it, we record **mock** exam sessions. You will answer a short practice quiz (20 to 30
minutes) on this computer. It has no effect on any grade, course or job.

### What we will ask you to do

You may be asked to behave normally, or to follow a short script. For example, you might be asked to
glance at a phone or read from paper notes when a message appears on screen. You will see each
instruction before it happens. Some scripts involve a second adult volunteer, who must give their own
consent.

### What we record

- Video and sound from the webcam and microphone during the session.
- One still photo at the start, used as a reference image of your face.
- Browser activity in the quiz page only: whether the quiz tab is visible, full-screen changes, when
  text is pasted and its length (not its content), the number of screens (where the browser reports
  it), and how many keys are typed per 10 seconds (not which keys).
- The session conditions the operator records (room lighting, webcam quality, room noise, whether
  you wear glasses or a head covering). These describe the recording conditions, not you.

We do **not** record anything outside the quiz page, other browser tabs, files on this computer, or
what you type.

### How the recordings are used

Automatic, pre-trained detectors will process the recordings to produce timestamped measurements,
such as "a face was visible" or "speech was detected". We use these measurements to study how often
the detectors are wrong, and to check that our simulated training data is realistic.

Information about you, such as an age band, is optional. If you choose to give it, we store it
separately. It is used only to check that the system treats groups fairly, and never as an input to
any model.

### Storage and deletion

- Recordings are stored on the project's secured storage and can be viewed only by the project team.
- Your name appears only on this consent record. Everywhere else you are identified by a code
  (for example P-014).
- All raw video, sound and photos are deleted at the end of the project, and no later than the
  retention date shown in the system. Only derived measurements (numbers and timestamps) are kept.

### Your right to withdraw

Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time during a session, and you may withdraw later
without giving a reason. If you withdraw, we delete your recordings, the measurements derived from
them, and any optional information you gave us. We keep a record that consent was given and later
withdrawn, with your name removed.

### Age

You must be 18 or older to take part.

### Agreement

By signing, you confirm that:

1. You agree to be recorded as described above.
2. You agree to the recordings being analysed as described above.
3. You understand how long recordings are kept and when they are deleted.
4. You understand that you can withdraw at any time and have your data deleted.
